## Critical scientific safeguards
1. A brand or product catalog record is not proof that the assay's output is quantitatively suitable for Deventer.
2. Exact kit name/catalog code, IFU revision, assay type, units, conversion equation, BTT and vaccine product/platform must match.
3. IDEXX-standard titer values or BTT must not be copied to another ELISA without validated conversion.
4. Conventional live IBD vaccine Deventer models must not be applied to HVT-VP2 vectors or immune-complex vaccines without a platform-specific validated model.
5. Deventer outputs are distributions/coverage decisions, not proof of protection; field pressure, uniformity, application quality and vaccine take matter.
6. Sample number and selection assumptions are protocol-specific. Guidance sources mention 18 minimum (some product instructions) and 23 for quantitative prediction; do not hardcode one universal number.
7. A 5% initial responder/coverage scenario is not universal; Elanco documents it as a default scenario with assumptions. 75% is a different scenario, not a universal default.
8. The current UI user-attestation QC gate is not automated laboratory QC and must be labelled accordingly.

## SIM Bio Lab verification checklist
Request/inspect photographs of all box sides and IFU:
- legal manufacturer and brand (SIM Bio Lab may be distributor/brand rather than manufacturer)
- exact product/SKU, intended species and specimen
- indirect/competitive ELISA vs antigen assay
- wavelength, sample dilution, incubation and controls
- qualitative vs quantitative designation
- OD-to-titer/S/P equation, units and interpretation
- lot-specific QC/acceptance criteria and IFU revision/date
- local registration/authorization and Iranian manufacturer address

Only after these are obtained should the item move from catalog-only to documented; clinical calculation still needs validation.
