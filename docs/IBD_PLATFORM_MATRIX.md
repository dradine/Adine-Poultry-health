# IBD vaccine platform routing matrix — evidence review

This document prevents the Deventer estimator from being applied to unlike vaccine technologies. It is not a country-specific product register and does not imply that any brand is currently registered or stocked in Iran.

| Platform | Biological mechanism | MDA interpretation | Model routing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conventional live attenuated | Replicating IBDV vaccine strain; vaccine take/replication and bursal response matter | Anti-IBDV MDA can neutralize or delay vaccine replication; product/strain BTT and assay scale matter | Deventer-style timing may be evaluated only for exact product + assay mapping |
| Immune-complex | Live IBDV strain complexed with specific antibodies; release/replication is delayed and product-designed | Complex-associated antibodies protect vaccine virus from MDA; product mechanism and release kinetics matter | Separate product-specific model; do not use conventional Deventer BTT as a substitute |
| HVT-VP2 vector | HVT vector expresses IBDV VP2 antigen; does not contain replicating live IBDV vaccine strain | Anti-VP2 MDA and anti-HVT vector immunity are biologically distinct; product claims and local labels govern | Separate vector/insert model; do not calculate a Deventer vaccination age |
| Inactivated | Non-replicating antigen with adjuvant; usually part of breeder/pullet priming/booster programme | Evaluate breeder immunity and offspring MDA as a programme, not as a live-vaccine break-through timing calculation | Separate breeder/booster programme model |

## Product-specific evidence examples — not universal constants

### Conventional live
- UK-authorised Avishield IBD Plus label (Genera Inc.; Vm 43676/3000; revised June 2024) specifies an intermediate-plus G6 live strain, drinking-water route, use from 10 days depending on MDA, BTT ≤500 IDEXX ELISA units, onset of immunity 2 weeks and duration 5 weeks. The project catalog stores this as a **GB-only documented label example**, linked to IDEXX IBD Ab Test part 99-09260; it is not a claim of Iranian registration or stock.
- Elanco AviPro IBD guidelines explain that BTT is vaccine-specific and can differ between ELISA kits. They describe field-pressure, MDA level and flock homogeneity as factors in programme selection.
- The German authorised product information for AviPro IBD Xtreme reports BTT 636 on an ELISA scale, recommends at least 20 birds for serology in that product context, and includes a Deventer-formula description. The label also states the birds should be at least 7 days old at vaccination. This is a product-and-market-specific example, not a generic BTT.

### Immune-complex
- Ceva's Transmune booklet describes a Winterfield 2512 IBD strain combined with virus-protecting immunoglobulins and reports delayed release of vaccine virus by approximately 7–10 days. This supports separate platform-specific modelling rather than inserting an ordinary live-vaccine BTT into Deventer.

### HVT-VP2 vector
- Boehringer Ingelheim describes VAXXITEK HVT+IBD as an HVT vector expressing VP2 and states that the product does not interfere with maternal antibodies. This is the manufacturer's product claim for its own product and label; it does not justify applying that claim to every vector or every field condition.
- The product information describes hatchery administration at day-old or in ovo; a Deventer field-age calculation is not the relevant timing method for this platform.

## Required product registry fields
- Country/market, registration number and label revision date
- Manufacturer, exact commercial name, vaccine strain/construct and platform
- Intended species/production type, route, dose and administration age window
- Exact BTT (only when defined), assay kit and version, conversion method and source
- Release kinetics or onset-of-immunity claims for immune-complex/vector products
- Label source URL, local regulatory source, document hash and reviewer/date
- Validation status: unverified → documented → locally validated → retired

## WOAH diagnostic framework and 2025 review
- WOAH Terrestrial Manual 2024 classifies ELISA as strongly recommended for assessing post-vaccination immune status, but the same chapter separates antibody detection from agent detection and virus characterisation. RT-PCR alone does not normally distinguish live vaccine strains from field isolates; virus characterisation/sequence and bursal pathology may be needed for that purpose.
- WOAH recognises conventional live attenuated, inactivated, recombinant VP2 and immune-complex vaccines as distinct categories. Live attenuated vaccines are classified by increasing invasiveness and ability to overcome MDA; immune-complex and recombinant vaccines allow hatchery administration even in the presence of MDA. This supports strict platform-specific model routing.
- The 2025 review of IBDV vaccines also distinguishes live, immune-complex, HVT-VP2 and inactivated/subunit platforms. It is a review, not a substitute for product SPCs or independent local field validation.

## Interpretation cautions
- Manufacturer brochures are product evidence, not independent comparative trials.
- Local registration and local package inserts take precedence over another country's product page.
- A claim such as "no interference with MDA" is not equivalent to proof of identical protection under all field conditions.
- Serology is not a substitute for vaccine-take monitoring, bursal histopathology, PCR/sequence differentiation or clinical/performance assessment.
